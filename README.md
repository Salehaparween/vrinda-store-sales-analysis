# 🛍️ Vrinda Store Annual Retail Performance Analysis (2022)

An end-to-end retail business intelligence project analyzing annual transaction data for Vrinda Store. This project combines data wrangling in Microsoft Excel with interactive visualization in Power BI to uncover sales trends, customer demographics, and channel performance.

---

## 📌 Project Objectives
The objective of this analysis is to evaluate Vrinda Store's annual retail performance to answer critical commercial questions:
1. **Demographic Profiling**: Who are the primary customer segments driving sales?
2. **Sales Channel Efficiency**: Which e-commerce channels (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, etc.) deliver the highest order volume?
3. **Regional Performance**: Which states contribute the most to gross revenue?
4. **Order Status**: What percentage of orders are successfully delivered vs. canceled or returned?

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Microsoft Excel**: Data cleaning, handling missing records, formatting dates, pivot tables, and categorization formulas.
* **Power BI**: Data modeling, custom DAX metrics, interactive filters, KPI cards, and dashboard design.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

* **Target Audience**: Women are the dominant buyer demographic, contributing **~65% of total purchases**.
* **High-Value Age Group**: Adults aged **30–49 years** contribute the highest revenue (~50% of total sales).
* **Top Contributing States**: **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh** represent the top 3 states by sales volume (accounting for ~35% of total orders).
* **Leading Channels**: **Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart** are the primary sales channels, driving over 80% of total revenue.
* **Delivery Success**: Over 92% of orders were successfully delivered, with cancellations and returns remaining under 8%.

---

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

1. **Targeted Female-Centric Campaigns**: Concentrate marketing and promotional spend on women aged 30–49 across social channels and search ads.
2. **Channel Optimization**: Prioritize inventory allocation and promotional tie-ups with Amazon and Myntra, as they yield the highest conversion rates.
3. **Regional Warehousing**: Consider regional logistics hubs in Maharashtra and Karnataka to reduce shipping lead times and lower return rates.
